## 2. Information we handle

Depending on how you use Plexshity, we may handle:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, account identifier, email-verification status, and account timestamps.
- **Authentication information:** your password is hashed before it is stored; we do not store it as readable text. When you sign in, we use an authentication cookie to keep you signed in.
- **Chats and prompts:** chat titles, messages you submit, AI responses, and associated timestamps. These are stored so you can view and manage your chat history.
- **Theme preference:** your selected theme is saved in your browser's local storage.
- **Operational information:** request and error logs used to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service. Our hosting providers may also process technical information such as network and device information when you connect.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure accounts and verify email addresses;
- provide chat, search, and AI-response features;
- store, display, and delete chat history at your direction;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and protect Plexshity; and
- respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

## 4. AI, search, and other service providers

To provide features, relevant information may be sent to providers that process it for us:

- **Google Gemini** processes chat context to generate responses.
- **OpenRouter** processes the first prompt in a conversation to generate a chat title.
- **Tavily** receives search queries when a request uses the web-search feature.
- **Google/Gmail** is used to deliver account-verification email.
- **Render, Vercel, MongoDB, and Redis hosting providers** support application hosting, storage, and authentication infrastructure.

These providers may process information on systems in countries other than yours and may have their own privacy terms. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive information in chat prompts. Before publishing this policy, confirm each provider's current data-retention and model-training settings for the specific plans and APIs in use.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

Plexshity uses an HTTP-only authentication cookie to maintain your signed-in session. The cookie expires after seven days; in production it is configured for secure transport. We also store your theme preference in browser local storage. These are used for service functionality, not to store your password.

## 6. How long we keep information

Chat history is kept until you delete it using the chat controls or until it is otherwise removed to meet legal, security, or operational requirements. Account information is retained while the account is active. The service does not currently provide a self-service account-deletion control; contact **[privacy contact email]** to request account deletion or access to your information.

When you log out, the authentication token is placed in the Redis logout blocklist for up to seven days to prevent reuse. Hosting and operational logs are retained according to the applicable provider's settings and terms.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can delete individual chats in the application and can change your theme preference in the application. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. To make a request, contact **[privacy contact email]**. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

## 8. Security

We use measures intended to protect information, including password hashing and authenticated access to account data. No service or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children

Plexshity is not intended for children who are below the minimum age to consent to online services in their location. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact **[privacy contact email]**.
